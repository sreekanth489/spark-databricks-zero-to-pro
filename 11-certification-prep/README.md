# Module 11: Certification Prep

> Prepare for the Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate and Professional exams with study guides, practice questions, and hands-on review exercises.

## Prerequisites

- Completed Modules 01 through 10 (or equivalent experience)
- Working Databricks workspace (Community Edition or higher)
- Familiarity with PySpark, Delta Lake, Structured Streaming, and Unity Catalog concepts

## Certification Paths

### Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Questions | 45 multiple choice |
| Duration | 90 minutes |
| Passing score | 70% (approximately 32 correct) |
| Cost | $200 USD |
| Validity | 2 years |
| Prerequisites | None (but 6+ months of hands-on experience recommended) |

This exam validates your ability to complete basic data engineering tasks using Databricks and Apache Spark. It tests foundational knowledge of the Lakehouse architecture, ELT with Spark SQL and Python, incremental data processing, production pipelines, and data governance.

### Databricks Certified Data Engineer Professional

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Questions | 60 multiple choice |
| Duration | 120 minutes |
| Passing score | 70% (approximately 42 correct) |
| Cost | $300 USD |
| Validity | 2 years |
| Prerequisites | Associate certification recommended |

This exam validates your ability to design, build, and maintain production data pipelines. It goes deeper into performance tuning, security, CI/CD, testing, monitoring, and advanced data modeling.

## How to Use This Module

1. **Read the exam guide** for your target certification to understand domains and weights.
2. **Work through the companion notebook** to reinforce key concepts with hands-on exercises.
3. **Take the practice questions** (guide) and code exercises (notebook) under timed conditions.
4. **Review the study plan** and cheat sheet notebook for final preparation.
5. **Revisit earlier modules** for any domains where you score below 70%.

## What You Will Be Able to Do After This Module

- Understand the format, domains, and passing criteria for both certification exams
- Identify your strongest and weakest knowledge areas through practice questions
- Write code that demonstrates proficiency in all tested exam domains
- Follow a structured study plan tailored to your target exam
- Access a quick-reference notebook of common operations for last-minute review

## Next Steps

After completing this module, schedule your exam through the Databricks Academy portal. Aim to take the exam within 1-2 weeks of finishing your preparation to keep knowledge fresh.
